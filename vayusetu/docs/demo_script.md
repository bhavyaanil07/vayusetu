# Demo video script (about 3 minutes)

Use this script to narrate the recorded demo, or to record your own screen capture after deploying.
To record: on Windows press Win + Alt + R (Xbox Game Bar); on Linux use OBS Studio or Kazam. Keep the browser at
100% zoom and hide the bookmarks bar.

| Time | Screen | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Title card | "India monitors air quality at city scale, so the smoke from a landfill fire or a night-shift furnace goes unseen. This is VayuSetu: a federated clean-air platform that finds hidden hotspots, forecasts spikes and alerts the right authority." |
| 0:15 | Command Center | "We're replaying real CPCB data from 30 October 2019, two days before Delhi declared a public health emergency. Delhi is already Severe, and VayuCast flags five cities along the Amritsar–Kolkata corridor at high spike risk. Satellite fire detections show crop burning upwind in Punjab, and the wind is carrying the smoke towards Delhi." |
| 0:40 | Key moment: 1 Nov | "Jump to 1 November, the emergency day, and the whole corridor turns red." |
| 0:50 | Hotspot Radar | "Now street level. Every ward has a low-cost citizen sensor. Coloured by local excess, a smoky day no longer hides everything: VayuSetu finds a landfill fire at Ghazipur, night-time furnace emissions at Bawana and Wazirpur, and waste burning. It explains each one: the fine-particle ratio says combustion, the time pattern says night, and citizen photos confirm it. Faulty sensors are quarantined automatically." |
| 1:25 | Spike Forecast | "The forecast gives 72 hours with honest uncertainty. In this replay we can reveal what actually happened. On a year of data the model never saw, it cut error by 9 to 11 percent against persistence. For Delhi it expects GRAP Stage III and lists the measures to pre-position." |
| 1:50 | Citizen Reports | "Citizens can report too. The photo is analysed on the spot: haze level with the dark channel prior, a smoke or fire category, and a credibility score checked against nearby sensors. The report then feeds hotspot detection." |
| 2:10 | Alerts & Response | "Every alert goes to the right desk. A Severe forecast goes to CAQM, the pollution committee and the disaster authority; a landfill fire goes to the municipal body and fire services. Each alert exports as CAP 1.2, the format of India's SACHET alert system." |
| 2:30 | Federated Network | "Finally, cities share models, not data. Nineteen cities trained one model together with federated learning. It cut error by 9 percent against each city training alone, and a new city with only two weeks of data gets a working model on day one. Here it is training live." |
| 2:55 | Closing card | "VayuSetu: citizen sensors, satellites and weather, turned into action. Thank you." |
